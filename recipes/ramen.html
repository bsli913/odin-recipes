Ramen

Description

Fast to make and delicous meal

Ingredients

  • Ramen noodles
  • Garlic and ginger
  • Broth
  • Dried mushrooms
  • Veggies

Steps

  1. Stir-Fry The Aromatics: Garlic and ginger, what a delicious duo.
  2. Add some chicken broth and dried shiitake mushrooms for some umami punch.
  3. Add Noodles: Cook your noodles right in the broth with some scallions (more flavor, please!).
  4. Add Veg: Thinly sliced kale, shredded carrots, whatever you’d like! Cook until just tender.
